jwsync

Combine your JW Library backups from several devices into one file that has everything from all of them.

Nothing is uploaded. Your backups are opened inside this page, on this device. They are never sent anywhere, and this page works with the internet switched off.

1 · Your backups

Choose the .jwlibrary backup you made on each device. You need at least two.

    2 · Combine them

    3 · Result

    Save it into the same folder your devices share, then on each device open JW Library and go to Personal Study, tap the ⤒ icon, and choose Restore Backup.
